Roadfy Privacy Policy

In effect since October 28, 2026.

This is a translation of the Portuguese original. If the two ever differ, the Portuguese version prevails.

Roadfy is an app for trail motorcyclists: you record your route by GPS, rate the stretch, attach photos and publish it for other riders to discover. Doing this requires collecting sensitive data — where you have been, and when. This page says exactly what is collected, what for, who it is shared with and how to delete everything.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018), is David Ramon Lopes Faustino, an individual and the developer of Roadfy.

Contact for any privacy matter, including exercising your rights: privacidade@roadforyou.com.

2. Location, even with the app closed

Read this part carefully — it is the most invasive one.

During a recording, Roadfy collects your precise location continuously and in the background: with the screen locked and even with the app closed. Without this the product would not exist — a trail is where the signal ends and the system closes apps to save battery, and a recording that stops halfway is useless.

Collection happens only while a recording is in progress, started by you and flagged by a permanent notification for as long as it lasts. Outside of that, the app does not track your position.

The permission is requested separately by the system and can be revoked at any time in your device settings. If you revoke it, recording stops working; the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Data we collect

To create and maintain your account

  • Email and password, when you sign up by email. The password is stored only as a cryptographic hash — we have no access to it.
  • Name, email and profile photo of your Google account, when you sign in with Google. We receive this data from Google at the moment you sign in.
  • Profile: username, display name, bio, motorcycle and photo, when you fill them in.

When you record and publish a ride

  • GPS points: latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy and the time of each point along the route.
  • Ride summary: distance, duration, elevation gain, start and finish.
  • Your ratings: difficulty, surface quality, scenery, surface types, title and any notes you write.
  • Photos you attach, from the camera or the gallery.

When you use the social features

  • Likes, comments, who you follow and who follows you.
  • Routes saved to the catalogue, routes you started riding and completions.
  • Groups you create or join, including chat messages.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report content, we keep what was reported, the reason you chose, the text you wrote and the date. When you block someone, we keep who you blocked and when.

Who sees the report you made: only you and us. It is not shown to the person reported, and we do not notify them. A report does not delete anything automatically — it goes into a list that we review by hand, which is why we do not promise a deadline.

Who sees a block: only you. The list of people you blocked is in Profile → Settings → Blocked riders (Pilotos bloqueados), and nobody else consults it. The effect, though, goes both ways: you stop showing up for each other in the feed, in comments and in search, and whatever either person had already published keeps existing for everyone else.

If you subscribe to Premium

  • Subscription status, plan and the end date of the period.
  • We do not receive or store your card data.
  • Premium is not on sale at the moment. When it comes back, billing will be done by Google Play, on the Google account you already use — and this policy will be updated before that, saying what Play tells us.

When something goes wrong in the app

If the app hits an unexpected error, it sends a technical report to the Sentry service, which we use to find and fix failures. This report contains the error message, the code's call stack, the device's model and system version, and the app version.

What this report does not contain, by design: your name, your email, your user identifier, any GPS coordinate and any photo. We also do not capture screenshots or session recordings — features the service offers and that we turned off, because this app's screen shows precisely the map with your position.

On your device, never leaving it

Recordings in progress and not yet published stay in a local database, together with the attached photos, so they survive a lack of signal and the app being closed. Your session is kept in the system's credential vault (Keychain on iOS, KeyStore on Android). None of this is sent until you publish, and all of it is erased when you delete the account or sign out.

5. What other people can see

This is the part that most often surprises people, so it is spelled out:

  • A ride published as public: your full track on the map, the summary, your ratings and the photos are visible to anyone signed in to the app. The track includes the point where the recording started and ended.
  • A ride marked as private: visible only to you, photos included.
  • Your profile: username, display name, bio, motorcycle and photo are visible to people who are signed in. The “private profile” option hides the lists of who you follow and who follows you — it does not hide the profile or the public rides.
  • Group chat: visible only to the participants of that group.

About the starting point. The start and the end of a recording are often the recorder's home. That is why, when publishing a public ride, the app cuts the first and the last 300 metres of the track before sending it. That stretch is not trimmed at display time: it never leaves your device, and so never reaches our servers or Google's. Not even we can see it.

The option is on the publishing screen and comes switched on. You can switch it off for a specific ride — for example, when the trail starts far from where you live and the whole route matters. With it off, the full track is sent.

Distance, duration and elevation gain remain those of the whole ride: they do not reveal where you were.

7. Where the data is kept

The Supabase servers we use are in Canada, and the other providers above operate in other countries. This means your data is transferred outside Brazil, based on art. 33 of the LGPD and on those providers' contractual safeguards.

8. How long we keep it

  • For as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete the account, the data is erased immediately and irreversibly — photos included.
  • One exception, and the reason for it: routes you added to the catalogue stay, without your name. Other people saved and completed those routes, and deleting them would delete the history of people who asked for nothing. The link to you is removed.
  • A second exception, for the same kind of reason: a report that another person made about your content stays, without the link to you. The complaint is the data of the person who complained, and deleting it together with your account would delete the record of someone who asked for nothing. Reports that you made are deleted with your account.
  • Payment records are kept for the period required by tax law.

9. How to delete your account

Inside the app, under Profile → Settings → Delete my account (Perfil → Configurações → Excluir minha conta). The screen shows what will be erased and asks for written confirmation.

If you have already uninstalled the app, use the account deletion page.

10. Your rights

The LGPD guarantees you, at any time and free of charge:

  • Confirmation of whether we process your data, and access to what we have
  • Correction of incomplete or outdated data
  • Anonymisation, blocking or deletion
  • Portability to another provider
  • Withdrawal of consent — including location consent, in your device settings
  • Information about who we share your data with

Write to privacidade@roadforyou.com. We reply within 15 days.

11. Security

Access to the data is controlled in the database itself, by rules that evaluate every read and every write individually: a private ride and its photos are not delivered to anyone but the owner, even if someone discovers the file's address. Connections are encrypted and your session is kept in the device's credential vault.

No system is unbreakable. If an incident occurs that brings a relevant risk to you, we will notify you and Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

12. Minimum age

Roadfy is intended for people over 18, because it deals with riding motorcycles on trails. We do not intentionally collect data from children and teenagers. If we become aware of a sign-up in that condition, the account will be deleted.

13. Changes to this policy

When we change something relevant, we will let you know in the app before the change takes effect, and the date at the top of this page will be updated.
