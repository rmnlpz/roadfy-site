Delete your Roadfy account

This is a translation of the Portuguese original. If the two ever differ, the Portuguese version prevails.

You can delete your account and everything it holds at any time. The deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no way to recover it afterwards, not even for us.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Roadfy and go to Profile (Perfil)
  2. Tap Settings (Configurações)
  3. Tap Delete my account (Excluir minha conta)
  4. Read what will be erased and type the confirmation phrase shown on the screen (EXCLUIR MINHA CONTA in the current version of the app) to confirm

If you have already uninstalled the app

Write to privacidade@roadforyou.com from the same email address registered on the account, with the subject “Delete my account”.

We ask for the same email as the account because that is how we confirm that the request is yours — we cannot delete someone's account from an address we do not know to be theirs. We complete it within 15 days and confirm by email when it is done.

What is erased

  • Your profile, your photo and your followers
  • Your rides, with the GPS points and the photos
  • Your likes, comments and saved routes
  • Your route completions
  • Your Premium subscription, if any
  • The groups you created, with their chat — for all participants
  • Everything the app stored on your device, including unpublished recordings

What stays, and why

The routes you added to the catalogue stay there, without your name. Other people saved and completed those routes: deleting them would delete the history of people who asked for nothing. Your link to them is removed.

Payment records, if you subscribed to Premium, are kept for the period that tax law requires.

Details about what we collect and why are in the Privacy Policy.
